# Drumbox (AudioFeel)

16-pad synthesized drum machine for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. Every sound is synthesized (no samples).

Pads (MIDI notes 36-51): Kick, Rim, Snare, Clap, Snare 2, Tom Lo, Closed Hat, Tom Mid, Shaker, Tom Hi, Open Hat, Conga, Cowbell, Crash, Clave, Ride.

Per pad: Tune, Decay, Tone, Level, Pan. Tone changes with the drum (kick: click and sweep, snare: noise against body, clap: filter colour, hats and cymbals: brightness, toms and conga: overtone, rim/cowbell/clave: pitch spread). Master: Volume, Drive (soft saturation), Vel Sens. Closed hat chokes open hat; all drums are one-shots (note-offs ignored).

Kits: Init Kit, 808 Deep, 909 Punch, Tight Techno, Lo-Fi Dust, Trap Hard, Electro Snap, Perc Jam, Soft Room. These are starting points made from my own recipes: they do not copy or emulate any instrument's samples.

Not implemented: multi-outputs, sample import, step sequencer, per-pad choke groups other than the hats.

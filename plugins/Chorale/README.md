# Chorale (AudioFeel)

A synthesized choir for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. 6 voices. Each voice is a stack of detuned saws plus breath noise through three parallel band-pass filters tuned to the first three formants of a vowel. **Vowel** morphs smoothly A - E - I - O - U; **Vowel Motion** lets a slow LFO move the vowel by itself, with a different phase per voice, for the drifting "ahh-ooh" of a real choir; **Singer** (Bass, Tenor, Alto, Soprano) scales the formants. A detuned stack and a slow vibrato that fades in give the unison its size. Chorus and a large reverb at the output.

- **Singing it**: velocity sets loudness, brightness and attack speed (and opens the vowel); mod wheel, aftertouch and the Dynamics knob make a crescendo; Onset starts each note with a breath, a flat pitch and a settling vowel; Humanize gives each note small offsets; Solo mode is one singer (last key wins, Glide between held keys).
- **VOICE**: Singer, Vowel, Vowel Motion, Motion Rate, Choir Size, Breath, Brightness, Vibrato. **ENV / FX**: Attack, Release, Bend Range, Volume, Chorus Mix, Reverb Mix / Size / Damp.

Programs: Ahh Choir, Solo Soprano, Solo Tenor, Ooh Choir, Cathedral, Epic Ahh, Male Choir, Boys Choir, Vowel Sweep, Slow Ooh-Ahh, Whisper, Eerie Voices, Requiem.

Honest limits: formant synthesis of vowels only, no consonants and no words; it is a choral pad, not a vocal sampler. The vowel table holds textbook averages and was judged by ear on one setup. Desktop builds have no custom UI; the MPC shows touch pages.

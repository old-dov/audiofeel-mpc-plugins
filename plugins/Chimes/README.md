# Chimes (AudioFeel)

Bells and tuned percussion for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. 8 voices, FM-synthesized, no samples. Each voice is a two-operator FM pair (a sine carrier phase-modulated by a sine at a fixed ratio, with an index that fades faster than the note, so the attack is bright and the ring pure) plus one inharmonic partial for the metal or wood of the bar.

- **Instrument**: Bell, Music Box, Celesta, Glockenspiel, Marimba, Tubular Bell, Vibraphone, Kalimba.
- **SOUND**: Instrument, Decay, Brightness (FM index), Hardness (how much velocity opens the index), Tremolo and Trem Rate (the vibraphone motor), Spread (stereo position by key), Release (long = a bell left to ring), Bend Range. **FX**: Volume, Chorus Mix, Reverb Mix / Size / Damp.

Programs: Init Bell, Church Bell, Music Box, Dream Celesta, Glockenspiel, Warm Marimba, Vibraphone, Kalimba, Frozen Bells, Wonder Tower, Soft Mallets.

Honest limits: FM imitations of bell and mallet sounds, judged by ear on one setup. Music Box and Glockenspiel sound an octave above the played note. No mallet noise layer, no real bar modes beyond one extra partial. Desktop builds have no custom UI; the MPC shows touch pages.

# Warbox (AudioFeel)

Cinematic percussion for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. 16 synthesized sounds on notes 36-51 (the pads of an MPC or an Ableton Drum Rack), no samples, one big reverb over the kit.

| Notes | Family | Sounds |
|---|---|---|
| 36-44 | Drums | Taiko Hi, Taiko Lo, War Drum, Frame Drum, Timpani Hi, Timpani Lo, Floor Tom, Epic Kick, Stomp |
| 45-47 | Metal | Anvil, Plate, Gong (slow swell, long ring) |
| 48-51 | Hits | Military Snare, Rimshot, Crash Swell, Taiko Rim |

- **Per sound**: Tune (+-12 st), Length (25-250 %), Tone (overtones / brightness / snap), Level, Pan.
- **Master**: Volume, Drive, Vel Sens, Rev Mix / Size / Damp.

Programs: Init Epic, Taiko Ensemble, Orchestral Timpani, War Machine, Gong & Metal, Tight Trailer, Dry Stage.

Not implemented: no drum rolls or flams, no choke groups, sounds are one-shots (note-off is ignored), the timpani are tuned by the Tune knob only (no pitch-bend). Desktop builds have no custom UI; the MPC shows touch pages.

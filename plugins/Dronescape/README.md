# Dronescape (AudioFeel)

Dark drones and evolving textures for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. 6 voices. Each voice stacks a sine sub, two detuned saws, an optional second pair of saws at an interval (fifth, fourth, octave, tritone, minor second, minor third: the tension-drone trick) and a band of tonal noise tuned to the note, through a two-pole low-pass kept moving by two slow LFOs, with a slow pitch drift per voice. Chorus and a long reverb at the output.

- **OSC**: Sub, Saw, Noise, Detune, Layer (interval), Layer Level, Drift, Bend Range.
- **FILTER / ENV**: Cutoff, Resonance, Evolve Rate / Depth, Attack, Release.
- **FX**: Chorus Mix, Reverb Mix / Size / Damp. **Master**: Volume.

Programs: Init Drone, Dark Drone, Abyss, Tension Bed, Tritone Dread, Wind Texture, Industrial Hum, Ice Field, Void, Slow Breath, Minor Cloud.

Not implemented: no velocity-to-filter, no sync to tempo for the LFOs. Desktop builds have no custom UI; the MPC shows touch pages.

# Brassline (AudioFeel)

A synthesized brass section and braams for Akai MPC standalone and VST2 hosts. 6 voices. Each voice is three detuned saws, a narrow pulse and (for the low sections) a sub saw, pushed through a Growl stage (soft saturation) into a low-pass that a filter envelope opens: Swell is how far the brass "blats" open as it speaks, and it speaks as slowly as the Attack. Scoop makes the note rise into place; the vibrato fades in late.

- **Playing it**: velocity sets loudness, brightness, growl and how fast the note speaks; mod wheel, aftertouch and the Dynamics knob open the brass up as it gets louder; Tongue adds the burst of the attack; Humanize gives each note small offsets; Mono is a solo line (last key wins, slurs between held keys, Glide).
- **Section**: Trumpet, Horn, Trombone, Tuba, Braam (the wide, detuned, heavily driven low stack of modern trailers).
- **BRASS**: Section, Attack, Swell, Brightness, Growl, Detune, Scoop, Vibrato. **ENV / FX**: Release, Bend Range, Volume, Chorus Mix, Reverb Mix / Size / Damp.

Programs: Init Brass, Solo Trumpet, Solo Trombone, Trumpet Section, French Horns, Trombones, Tuba Low, Braam, Braam Wide, Fanfare, Soft Brass Swell, Brass Stab, Dark Horns.

Honest limits: a synth that imitates brass, not a sample library; good for pads, swells, stabs and braams, less for exposed solo lines. No key-switching, no real legato transitions, no mute or flutter-tongue. Desktop builds have no custom UI; the MPC shows touch pages.
